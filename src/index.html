About me

I’m a retrained Product Designer with most of my experience in enterprise web design.

I started design inspired by stories of how designers shape a better world, blending creativity, empathy and hands-on problem-solving. A few yers in the field of product design, I can see the rise of AI reshaping the role I once imagined. What a priviledge it is to be experiencing these fascinating times, absolutely worth the ride.

The road ahead

I want to drive meaningful impact by crafting thoughtful, user-centered solutions, amplified by AI to accelerate iteration, validate ideas and stay focused on what truly matters: solving the right problems.

Enterprise UX

Enterprise design is less about trends and more about clarity. It’s about solving complex problems efficiently, making steady progress, and delivering real value, even in slow-moving, high-stakes environments.

Things I manage working in enterprise:

  • Navigate legacy systems and technical constraints
  • Design for users with highly specialized workflows
  • Collaborate with engineers and product managers to bring designs to life
  • Handle shifting priorities and trade-offs to move designs forward
  • Embrace incremental delivery over chasing perfection

The design process I aim to follow:

  • Learn
    Gather information, leverage all possible sources before forming an opinion.
  • Understand
    Listen and observe. Consider the context and make sure not to just build faster horses.
  • Ideate
    Other people will know other things. Not jumping straight to the solution. Define a shared goal.
  • Build
    A solid UX spec is key. Sometimes I’ll start from the north star and work down to an MVP.
  • Improve
    My love goes to the human, not the design. I’ll test, validate, measure and improve.
