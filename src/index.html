About me

I’m a retrained Product Designer with most of my experience in enterprise web design.

I started design inspired by stories of how designers shape a better world by blending creativity, empathy and hands-on problem-solving. A few yers in the field, I can see the rise of AI reshaping the role I once imagined. Fascinating times, absolutely worth the ride.

The road ahead

As a designer I want to stay focused on what truly matters: solving the right problems. I want to drive meaningful impact by crafting user-centered solutions, amplified by AI to accelerate iteration and validate ideas. Continuously learning.

Enterprise UX

I've been primarily working in the B2B landscape.
Enterprise design is less about trends and more about clarity. It’s about solving complex problems efficiently, making steady progress and delivering real value in environments that are slow-moving and high-stakes. Designing for enterprise has given me a solid foundation to grow as a designer.

Vibing

Designing by vibe helps me broaden my scope as a UI/UX designer. Exploring the consumer world where things are more about the energy, design is bold, emotional and fast-moving. I try to mix in some structure to create products that feel as good as they function.

Enterprise UX

Delivering steady value and improving workflows without disrupting what already works. Mainly for web interfaces.

Skills I've grown in enterprise:


  • Understanding specialized workflows: designing for highly technical users with unique needs and expertise.
  • Working within constraints: balancing modern design approaches with technical limitations and legacy systems.
  • Managing shifting priorities: adapting to changing business needs while making thoughtful trade-offs.
  • Focusing on incremental delivery: driving progress through practical solutions instead of chasing perfection.
  • Collaborating across disciplines: partnering closely with engineers and product managers to align design, technical feasibility, and business goals.
  • Navigating complexity: simplifying intricate processes without oversimplifying the reality of enterprise work.
  • Advocating for quality: ensuring details like accessibility, usability, and error handling are part of the conversation.
  • Communicating clearly: translating design decisions for diverse stakeholders across teams and functions.

Enterprise user's wants:


  • Diverse data points, all at a glance.
  • Simplified complexity, but keep advanced configuration capabilities handy.
  • Investigate issues in depth without leaving the interface.
  • Monitoring, debugging and analysis, all in one place.
  • Contextual guidance, but don’t make it feel like onboarding forever.
  • Improvements, but no disruptions of familiar workflows.
  • Alignment to other familiar tools and interaction patterns.

The design process I (usually) follow:


  1. Learn Gather information, leverage all possible sources without forming an opinion yet.
  2. Understand Listen and observe. Consider the context and make sure not to just build faster horses.
  3. Ideate Other people will know other things. Avoid to jump straight to the solution.
  4. Build A solid UX spec is key. Sometimes I’ll start from the north star and work down to an MVP.
  5. Improve My love goes to the human, not the design. I’ll test, validate, measure and improve.
